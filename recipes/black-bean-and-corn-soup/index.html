Black Bean and Corn Soup

Makes 4 bowls · About 30 minutes

Ingredient checklist

Cooking instructions

  1. Warm the oil in a soup pot over medium heat. Cook onion and jalapeño for 4–5 minutes until soft. Add garlic, cumin, oregano and chili powder. Stir for 30 seconds.
  2. Add beans, corn, tomatoes with their juices, and broth. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer uncovered for 15 minutes.
  3. Mash about a cup of beans against the side of the pot to thicken the soup. Stir in lime juice and cilantro. Taste before adding salt, then ladle into bowls and add toppings.
