Citrus-Garlic Steak Marinade

For 1½–2 lb steak · Serves 4

Ingredient checklist

Cooking instructions

  1. Whisk the juices, oil, soy sauce, garlic, spices and cilantro. Coat the steak in a shallow dish or zip-top bag. Cover and refrigerate.
  2. For skirt or flank, marinate 2–6 hours. For strip or ribeye, marinate 30 minutes to 2 hours.
  3. Heat a heavy skillet or grill over medium-high heat. Discard used marinade, brush off loose garlic and herbs, and pat the steak dry.
  4. Sear on both sides. For a thick steak, lower the heat after browning. Cook to 145°F in the thickest part, then rest on a clean board for at least 3 minutes.
  5. Slice thinly across the grain and serve with roasted salsa verde.
