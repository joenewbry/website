Roasted Salsa Verde

Makes about 1½ cups · Roast 18–22 minutes

Ingredient checklist

Cooking instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Husk the tomatillos and rinse off the sticky coating. Halve the peppers lengthwise, remove stems and scrape out the ribs and seeds for a milder sauce.
  2. Toss tomatillos, peppers, onion and unpeeled garlic with oil and salt on a rimmed sheet pan. Set peppers cut-side down. Roast 18–22 minutes, until the tomatillos slump and pepper skins blister.
  3. Let the vegetables cool until lukewarm. Slip the garlic from its skins. Transfer the vegetables and all the pan juices to a blender.
  4. Add cilantro, a squeeze of lime and optional avocado or seasoning. Pulse to your preferred texture. Taste and add more lime or salt. Spoon alongside sliced steak.
